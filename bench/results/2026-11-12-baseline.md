## Conditions and notes

- Command: `python3 bench.py --browsers chrome,ladybird --runs 5 --label baseline`, started 2026-11-12 07:54, about 1 minute.
- Ladybird `b1476b89` Release build plus `ladybird-screenshot-on-load.patch` (viewport screenshot at load; see README.md).
  Chrome is `chrome-headless-shell` 157.0.8103.51.
- Every run of both browsers exited 0 with a screenshot (70/70 each).
- Other builds ran at background priority on the efficiency cores. Load average 3.8 at the start.
- Ladybird's low SSIM on `plain` (0.59) and `wikipedia` (0.65) is real: different line heights and a
  ~25 px vertical offset shift every line of text, and SSIM punishes shifts hard.
- An earlier attempt the same morning was discarded: Ladybird used a closed DNS port and waited 5 s for a
  DNS timeout on `craigslist`, `kranblog` and `kranpost`. `serve.py` now answers NXDOMAIN at once.
